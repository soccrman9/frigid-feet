# Wayfinder Map: Frigid Feet 50K Relay App

Label: `wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A build-ready product and technical specification (covering UX flows, domain model, data schema, architecture, and a rapid phased implementation plan) for the **Frigid Feet 50K Relay** mobile-first web app and Basecamp Dashboard — built for under 30 friends, a \$0 hosting budget, an encouraging non-competitive vibe, and an early-January 2027 race date.

## Notes

- **Domain**: Small friend-group (~<30 Participants) 50K relay out of a single **Basecamp** — Teams run a 5K every hour across 10 **Rounds** to reach 50K. Both **Runners** and **Supporters** attend. Encouraging, non-competitive tone: help nervous or slower runners feel welcome and celebrate participation with simple team milestones and playful **Badges**.
- **Hard constraints**:
  - **Timeline**: Race date is in **early January 2027** (~6 weeks from charting). Favor speed-to-build and simplicity over over-engineering.
  - **Budget**: **\$0** (free-tier hosting, database, and APIs only).
  - **Platform**: Mobile-first web app + read-only **Basecamp Dashboard** (for a laptop/TV at Basecamp).
  - **Fitness apps prioritized**: Strava, Apple Health, and Google Fit (plus standard GPX/manual fallbacks).
- **Skills to consult**: Consult `grilling` and `domain-modeling` on grilling tickets (keep [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md) updated inline); `research` on research tickets; `prototype` and `modern-web-guidance` on UI/web prototype tickets.
- **Tracker**: Local Markdown (`.scratch/frigid-feet-50k/map.md` and `.scratch/frigid-feet-50k/issues/NN-<slug>.md`). No git repository/remote is configured yet, so research ticket findings are saved to `.scratch/frigid-feet-50k/research/<slug>.md` with a pointer from the ticket.

## Decisions so far

<!-- the index: one line per closed ticket, enough to judge relevance, then zoom the link for the detail the ticket holds -->

- [Fitness App API Feasibility for a Zero-Cost Web App](./issues/01-fitness-app-api-feasibility.md): Skip direct Strava, Apple Health, and Google Fit/Health Connect APIs (Strava bans displaying API data to other users under Section 2.3 and requires paid subscription + manual review; Apple HealthKit and Android Health Connect are native-only; Google Fit shuts down at the end of 2026) — favor manual/timer Leg logging, optional client-side GPX/TCX/FIT upload, a self-hosted static 5K `.gpx`, and an optional outbound Strava Club link.
- [Mobile Web Push, Live Location, and Offline Capabilities](./issues/02-web-push-gps-offline-capabilities.md): Standards-based Web Push (VAPID) works at \$0 as an opt-in Round reminder (iOS 16.4+ requires Add to Home Screen); live background browser GPS is blocked when screen-locked on iOS and Android (favor Basecamp check-ins + pace ETAs); handle spotty signal with Service Worker caching plus an idempotent IndexedDB queue flushed on foreground/reconnect (iOS Safari lacks Background Sync).
- [Zero-Cost Hosting and Realtime Backend Options](./issues/03-zero-cost-stack-options.md): Supabase Free + Cloudflare Pages minimizes 6-week build time at \$0/no-card with Postgres, Auth, Realtime (200 connections), and `pg_cron` + Edge Functions (just mitigate Supabase's 7-day inactivity pause and 2-email/hr built-in SMTP cap); Firebase Spark blocks Cloud Functions/Scheduler without upgrading to Blaze, while Cloudflare Workers/D1/SQLite Durable Objects (now free with zero pausing) require DIY auth and WebSocket sync.
- [Relay Rules, Hourly Round Schedule, and Leg Mechanics](./issues/04-relay-rules-and-round-mechanics.md): 10 independent hourly mass-start Rounds (default 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, ending 6:00 PM, no rolling baton, no >60-min penalty); Teams of 1–10 Runners with pre-scheduled Legs, unlimited/back-to-back Legs, anytime swaps, placeholder make-up Legs, no buddy-running feature, and walk-ins joining directly as Runners; 1–3 named 5K Courses (Runner's choice per Leg); data scoped by **Event** (renamed from Edition) for multi-year reuse.
- [Access, Identity, and Privacy Model for Friends](./issues/05-access-identity-and-privacy.md): Private Event gate auto-unlocked by a shareable invite URL / QR code; honor-system tap-your-name Participant profiles scoped fresh per Event (zero PINs/passwords); two roles only (**Participant** + **Organizer** via a shared Organizer passcode, no Team Captain); teammates and Organizers can log Legs on a Runner's behalf; individual chip-in amounts are Organizer-only while aggregate chip-in progress and all other Event data are open to friends; read-only Basecamp Dashboard via an unlisted secret-token URL.
- [Pre-Race Info, RSVP, Amenities Sign-Up, and Chip-In Flow](./issues/06-pre-race-info-rsvp-amenities.md): Structured Event Info hub + Organizer Announcements feed + in-app `.gpx` Course map previews (with `.gpx` download and optional external links); 4 RSVP statuses (`Runner`, `Supporter`, `Maybe`, `Can't make it`), optional Round availability window, and optional rough 5K pace / *"first 5K"* tag / dietary notes; hybrid **Supply** sign-up (Organizer wish list with slot counts + free-form entries + dietary summary banner); Organizer-only chip-in ledger driving a zero-pressure friend-facing progress card with copyable Venmo/Zelle handles; simple `Home` / `Teams` / `Race Day` / `Supplies` navigation.
- [Hybrid Team Formation and Free-Agent Matching](./issues/07-team-formation-and-matching.md): Lightweight self-service Team creation (name, optional tagline/color, Open/Closed toggle, optional note) with zero invite codes; one-tap direct join onto Open Teams (showing uncovered Rounds) + visible Free Agents list (`Going (Runner)` only; Teams are Runner-only) + Organizer placement/overrides; one Team per Runner (leaving unassigns future Legs, preserves completed Legs, and auto-deletes empty Teams with 0 completed Legs); 10-row Round 1–10 schedule builder with uncovered-Round cues and non-blocking warnings for availability mismatches or back-to-back Rounds; rosters stay unlocked through race day.
- [Encouraging Results, Simple Team Milestones, and Playful Badges](./issues/08-results-and-playful-badges.md): Non-competitive Team progress (`km / 50 km`, `Rounds / 10`, 10-Round bar, Event-wide km counter, alphabetical order) with individual Leg times hidden by default ("Completed 5K" only) unless a Runner opts in via a profile toggle to show times and join a separate individual **Speed Leaderboard** (viewable by all Participants, no Team speed rankings); live auto-calculated Team/Participant **Badges** + Organizer-awarded preset/custom superlatives (no peer voting); in-app Event Recap with optional Organizer note and one-tap **Copy Event Recap** (group chat) + **Copy My Strava Post** buttons (no PNG cards in v1).
- [Race-Day Runner Tracking, Timing, and Notification Strategy](./issues/09-race-day-tracking-and-notifications.md): Hybrid Leg tracking (Organizer **"Start Round N"** with quick-uncheck for late starters + individual Start/Finish/"Mark Completed" taps scoped to Runner/teammates/Organizers, primary Course default editable anytime, ~8s Undo toast + anytime status revert); skip live browser GPS for `Out on Course` + elapsed time + rough pace ETA (with a calm dashboard and gentle 60-min mobile *"Forgot to check in?"* nudge); skip Web Push for v1 in favor of in-app countdowns + Basecamp Dashboard "On Deck" banner and optional muted-by-default T-5/T-0 chime; pragmatic Service Worker read cache + IndexedDB client-timestamped check-in queue.
- [Fitness App Integration Scope and User Flows](./issues/10-fitness-app-integration-scope.md): Skip all direct fitness-app APIs/OAuth and per-Leg `.gpx`/`.tcx`/`.fit` file uploads for v1; support an optional pasted `https://` external activity URL on completed Legs, a collapsible "How to load this Course" GPX help tip next to Course `.gpx` downloads, a low-key Event Info Strava Club link, and neutral **"Copy My Run Summary"** copy so non-fitness-app Participants have a complete first-class experience.
- [Basecamp Dashboard and Mobile Race-Day UI Prototype](./issues/11-basecamp-dashboard-and-mobile-ui-prototype.md): Mobile Race-Day View uses **Variant B** (horizontal 10-Round pill rail, chronological Round lineup, alphabetical Team 50K bars, and a sticky bottom thumb-zone action dock) with light/dark theme options; Basecamp Dashboard uses **Variant A** (50/50 split airport board with Out on Course + On Deck countdown on the left, alphabetical Team 50K bars on the right, and a toggleable bottom Badges ticker) with light/dark theme options.
- [Tech Stack Selection and 6-Week Build Architecture](./issues/12-tech-stack-and-architecture.md): Single-repo **Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS** PWA (`vite-plugin-pwa`, `idb`, Leaflet + OSM) hosted on **Cloudflare Pages** (`*.pages.dev`, private GitHub repo) backed by **Supabase Free** (Postgres + Realtime + Anonymous Auth + `SECURITY DEFINER` unlock RPCs + `session_grants` RLS, zero Edge Functions); Course `.gpx` stored in Postgres `TEXT`; 3-day GitHub Actions cron heartbeat prevents 7-day pausing; in-app Organizer JSON/CSV snapshot export for backups; 2-phase launch on one complete Phase 1 schema.
- [Canonical Data Model, Postgres Schema, and RLS Access Rules](./issues/13-canonical-data-model-and-rls.md): 13 Event-scoped Postgres tables deployed complete in Phase 1 (isolating hashed secrets in zero-RLS `event_secrets`, pre-seeding 10 `rounds` per Event and 10 `legs` per Team, computing auto-Badges client-side while storing Organizer superlatives, unifying `supply_items` + `supply_claims`, and protecting `chip_ins` behind Organizer-only RLS with a `get_chip_in_summary` aggregate RPC), paired with `session_grants` scope-based RLS and atomic RPCs (`unlock_*`, `rotate_event_secret`, `leave_team`, `start_round`, `create_event`).
- [Realtime Sync and Offline Check-In Reconciliation Design](./issues/14-realtime-sync-and-offline-reconciliation.md): Single multiplexed `postgres_changes` channel per active tab (mobile pauses after ~30s background + foreground resync; Basecamp Dashboard stays connected with reconnect resync + 60s degraded-mode fallback poll); Workbox `CacheFirst` app shell + IndexedDB `event_snapshot` offline hydration; Leg-only IndexedDB `leg_mutation_queue` flushed FIFO on `online`/`visibilitychange`/`focus`/15s backoff + manual "Sync now" pill; idempotent `apply_leg_mutation` RPC (`last_mutation_id`, `client_updated_at` LWW, Organizer precedence, no-regress `completed` guard) with local-cancel or compensating-revert ~8s Undo.
- [Final Build-Ready Specification and Phased Launch Plan](./issues/15-final-spec-and-phased-launch-plan.md): Consolidated all 15 decisions into [`docs/SPEC.md`](../../docs/SPEC.md) (paired with `GLOSSARY.md` and the Race-Day HTML prototype), locked URL routing (`/` → `/e/:slug`, `/e/:slug?code=...` stripped after unlock, `/dashboard/:token`) and 4-tab view placement, tightened `start_round` timestamp sync / confirmation guard / auto-Badge rules, and finalized the 6-week 2-phase rollout (Phase 1 live Dec 7, Phase 2 live Dec 28, Week 6 6-point dry-run checklist).

## Out of scope

- **In-app payment processing (Stripe, checkout, refunds)**: Friends chip in off-app (Venmo/Zelle) to cover facility, food, water, and first aid; the app only displays payment instructions and optional organizer-managed paid status.
- **Legal waiver management & commercial chip-timing (RFID) hardware**: Unnecessary for an informal <30-person friend event.
- **Multi-organizer / multi-tenant SaaS features**: Purpose-built for the Frigid Feet 50K Relay (single organizer, optionally repeatable by **Event**).
- **Paid SMS or transactional email notification services**: Conflicts with the \$0 budget (and Web Push VAPID is deferred for v1).
- **Direct third-party fitness-app OAuth/APIs & workout file uploads (v1)**: Skipped for v1 per [Fitness App Integration Scope and User Flows](./issues/10-fitness-app-integration-scope.md) (eliminating third-party OAuth registration/credential setup).
- **Implementation of the application itself**: This map ends at the build-ready specification and implementation plan; coding starts after handoff.
