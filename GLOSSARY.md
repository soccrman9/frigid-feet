# Winter Grind 50K

A friendly, non-competitive 50K relay race for a small group of friends, run out of a single Basecamp where teams complete a 5K every hour for 10 hours.

## Language

**Event**:
A single dated running of the Winter Grind 50K (e.g., Winter Grind 50K 2027).
_Avoid_: edition, year, instance, race

**Basecamp**:
The single shared facility where every hourly 5K starts and finishes, and where shared Supplies and facility amenities live.
_Avoid_: hub, venue, HQ

**Course**:
A named 5K route starting and ending at Basecamp that a Runner can choose for a Leg during an Event.
_Avoid_: route, loop, track

**Participant**:
Anyone attending an Event, in either a Runner or Supporter role.
_Avoid_: user, guest, attendee

**Runner**:
A Participant who runs one or more 5K Legs for a Team.
_Avoid_: racer, athlete, player

**Supporter**:
A Participant attending to cheer, help with logistics, or hang out at Basecamp without running a Leg.
_Avoid_: spectator, volunteer, non-runner

**Organizer**:
A host of an Event with elevated permissions to manage Event details, Basecamp info, Courses, chip-in records, timing overrides, and official Badges.
_Avoid_: admin, race director, host

**Team**:
One to ten Runners collaborating to complete 10 hourly 5K Legs (50K total) during an Event.
_Avoid_: squad, crew, group

**Free Agent**:
A Runner who has not yet joined a Team and is available to be matched onto a partial Team or paired with other Free Agents.
_Avoid_: solo entrant, unassigned runner

**Round**:
One of the 10 hourly 5K intervals (Round 1 through Round 10) that make up an Event.
_Avoid_: hour, lap, wave

**Leg**:
A single Runner's 5K run for their Team within a specific Round.
_Avoid_: lap, segment, stint

**Badge**:
A playful superlative or simple team milestone awarded to a Participant or Team during or after an Event.
_Avoid_: accolade, trophy, prize

**Supply**:
A bringable item for Basecamp (such as food, drinks, or first-aid/cold-weather gear) that Participants can claim from an Organizer wish list or volunteer to bring.
_Avoid_: potluck item, amenity, donation
